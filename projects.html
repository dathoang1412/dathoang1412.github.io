GU.AI — AI Fashion Photography Platform

full-stack saas · team of 3 · 2026

A production platform of three apps — a Next.js customer web, a Node.js / Express API of ~98 endpoints, and an admin panel — letting fashion sellers make product photos and videos through nine AI tools (virtual try-on, model generation, editing) powered by Fashn.ai.

Credit-based billing runs on PayOS with HMAC-verified webhooks; Google Gemini drives an in-studio assistant with RAG semantic search — 768-dim embeddings indexed in pgvector / HNSW on PostgreSQL. Real-time notifications and support chat run on Socket.IO, behind a five-tier rate-limiter guarding the paid AI quota, with an RBAC admin for live monitoring and revenue analytics.

ii.

HistoryTalk — Interactive Vietnamese History

full-stack · team of 4 · 2026

A platform for learning Vietnamese history by talking to it: users voice-chat with RAG-powered historical characters (Azure Speech), take quizzes, and keep streaks through gamification — daily quests and an Android home-screen widget. I bootstrapped the Node.js / Express / MongoDB backend and the React Native (Expo) app from scratch, alongside a Next.js web client.

Under the hood: an OCR PDF-ingestion pipeline (Tesseract.js, 150-page documents with print-artifact filtering) feeding the RAG store, PayOS payments over a custom HMAC-SHA256 REST client, Firebase Cloud Messaging push, and Google OAuth — across a 154-endpoint, 19-model REST API.
